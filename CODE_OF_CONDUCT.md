# Code of conduct

Rollcall is a small project run in spare time. Everyone who opens an issue, comments or sends a pull request is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant, networking or GitHub.
- Assume good intent. A short or blunt message is usually someone busy, not someone rude.
- Keep criticism about the code and ideas, not the person.
- Accept that not every request will be built, and that "not right now" isn't personal.
- Respect people's privacy: don't post anyone's personal details, or details of someone else's network, without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, email or details of their network.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing for something after it's been answered.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, please use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (the ⋯ menu), which reaches GitHub's moderators, and/or let the maintainer, [shoey79](https://github.com/shoey79), know. Reports are treated in confidence.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
